What the Cambridge Fire Department Does

The Cambridge Fire Department is an all-hazards emergency response organization dedicated to protecting lives and property throughout Cambridge. Its work includes fire suppression and prevention, emergency medical services, public fire-safety education, hazardous-materials response, emergency preparedness, and specialized technical rescue. Department personnel also train for high-angle, structural-collapse, trench, confined-space, and water and ice rescue operations, helping the city prepare for and respond to a wide range of emergencies and disasters.
